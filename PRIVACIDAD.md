# Privacy / Privacidad

**English** · [Español](#español) · [Deutsch](#deutsch) · [Français](#français) · [Italiano](#italiano) · [Português](#português)

Folder2Collage is designed to keep your images private.

- **Nothing is ever sent from your PC.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry and no accounts. Your screenshots, your collages and your searches never leave your computer.
- **Everything runs locally.** Reading images, the AI analysis and the collage generation all happen on your machine.
- **The only internet connection** is the optional, one-time download of the AI model (about 130 MB from Hugging Face), and only if you turn on "similar photos". It only downloads: your images are never uploaded. After that, it works fully offline.
- **What is stored on your PC**, unencrypted, and never shared: your collages (in `Pictures\Collages` by default), the settings and the image-analysis cache (in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Folder2Collage`).
